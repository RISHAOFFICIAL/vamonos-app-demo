Vámonos! Detroit
App Concept

A native iOS app that brings healthy food, fitness classes, and community events together in one place. No more jumping between links.

  • Order Food: Smoothies, wraps, salads, aguas frescas. Pickup or DoorDash.
  • Book Classes: Zumba, Baila Fitness, Heels, Booty Camp, Yoga, and more.
  • Events: Art shows, workshops, mercaditos, community markets.
  • Loyalty: Earn points with every order. Free smoothie at 200 points.
  • Bilingual: English and Spanish, just like the community.

Try it: Tap the tabs at the bottom. Add items to your cart. Book a class. It's fully interactive!
